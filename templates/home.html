How we work

From first call to launch in four steps.

  1. 01

    Discover Day 1

    A call to understand your business, your customers and what the site has to do. You get a written scope and price.

  2. 02

    Plan Days 2–4

    Pages, features and milestones agreed in writing, with a price and a delivery date you can hold us to.

  3. 03

    Build Weeks 1–3

    We code it, connect payments, forms and AI, and you follow the progress live in your client dashboard.

  4. 04

    Launch Ongoing

    Domain, hosting and speed checks, then handover with training. Support plans keep it updated after launch.
